About Crackle

Crackle started as a vibe-coding passion project — an experiment in building a real, working tool from scratch while learning software engineering along the way.

Right now, it's built with fresh-roast coffee roasters in mind — for anyone tracking their own home or small-batch roasts who wants a simple way to log profiles and results as they go.

Thanks for checking it out! This is very much a work in progress, so if something doesn't work right, or you've got an idea that would make it more useful, I'd genuinely love to hear about it.
